2. What CastTV does

CastTV lets you send videos from your Android phone to compatible TVs on the same Wi-Fi network: videos stored on your phone and video links you choose. Today CastTV supports Google Cast (Chromecast, Google TV and TVs with Chromecast built-in) and DLNA/UPnP TVs and players. CastTV does not provide, host or sell any video content.

Some TVs cannot play some formats or show subtitles. The help center in the app explains what is supported. Please check that CastTV works with your TV in the free version before you buy Premium.

6. The free version

  • Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, sometimes a full-screen ad after a cast ends, and short ads you choose to watch.
  • Free casting limit. Each video can be cast for 15 minutes of playing time (the app shows the current value before casting). Then the video pauses and you can watch a short ad to get more minutes, buy Premium, or stop. If no ad can be shown, the app gives a short extra time so you are never stuck.
  • Subtitles. Free users can add subtitles to a video after watching a short ad.

We may change the number of free minutes, the extra minutes per ad and how often ads appear. The app always shows the current values.

7. CastTV Premium subscriptions

What you get. CastTV Premium is an optional subscription. It removes ads, removes the free casting limit, and lets you add subtitles to any video and adjust their timing. The free version stays usable without it.

Plans and price. Premium is offered as a weekly or a yearly subscription, at the price shown in the app and in Google Play before you buy, in your currency, with taxes included where required. There is no lifetime or one-time purchase.

Free trial. The yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Google Play decides who is eligible, and the app shows the trial only if you are. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and you are charged at the end of the trial.

Billing. All payments are made through Google Play and charged to your Google account. We never see your card or payment details.

Auto-renewal. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period (every week or every year) at the then-current price, until you cancel it.

How to cancel. Cancel anytime in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > CastTV Premium > Cancel subscription. You can also use Settings > Manage subscription in CastTV, which opens the same page. Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds. Purchases are processed by Google Play and refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy. You can request a refund from Google Play in your Google account, or write to us and we will help where Google Play allows it. Nothing in these terms limits refund rights you have under the law of your country.

Price changes. If we change the price of a subscription, Google Play informs you before the new price applies, as its rules require, and in some cases asks for your agreement. If you do not want to pay the new price, you can cancel before it applies.

Restore. "Restore purchases" in the app gives Premium back on a new phone or after a reinstall, with the same Google account.

Changes to Premium. We may improve or change Premium features. We will not remove a feature you paid for during a period you have already paid.
