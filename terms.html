2. What Cast TV does

Cast TV lets you send videos from your Android phone to compatible TVs on the same Wi-Fi network: videos stored on your phone and video links you choose. Today Cast TV supports Google Cast (Chromecast, Google TV and TVs with Chromecast built-in) and DLNA/UPnP TVs and players. Cast TV does not provide, host or sell any video content.

6. A free app with ads

  • No purchases. Cast TV is free. It has no subscription and nothing to buy in the app. Premium subscriptions are not available.
  • Ads. Cast TV shows ads from Google AdMob: banners at the bottom of some screens, cards labeled "Ad", sometimes a full-screen ad after a cast ends, sometimes an ad when you come back after several hours away, and short ads you choose to watch for coins or more casting time. Never during a video on your TV.
  • Free casting limit. Each video can be cast for 13 minutes of playing time (the app shows the current value before casting). Then the video pauses and you can watch a short ad to get more minutes, or disconnect.

We may change the number of free minutes (13 today), the extra minutes per ad and how often ads appear. The app always shows the current values.

7. Coins and rewards

What coins are. Coins are a free reward inside Cast TV. They are a virtual item with no monetary value: they are not money, cannot be bought or sold, cannot be exchanged for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or anything outside the app, and cannot be transferred to another person or another phone.

How you get coins. Welcome coins the first time you open the app, a small bonus the first time you open it each day, and one coin each time you choose to watch a short ad in the Coins screen, up to a daily number of ads and a maximum balance shown in the app. The reward is stated before each ad. You can close an ad at any time; the coin is given when it ends. If no ad can be shown, you get the coin anyway, within the same daily number.

What coins unlock. Playlists (a queue of videos, songs and links that play one after another, with shuffle and repeat), the sleep timer and subtitles with timing, each for 24 hours, or a time without banners and full-screen ads (30 minutes), at the number of coins shown in the app. One video or song at a time, links and photo slideshows never need coins. When a video's free casting time is over, you can watch a short ad to continue without coins.

Stored on your phone only. Coins and unlocks are kept only on your phone. They are lost if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, and we cannot restore them.

Changes. We may change how many coins you get, the prices, the durations and how often ads appear, or end the coins feature. The app always shows the current values. Coins have no value and give no right to any payment.
