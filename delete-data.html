CastTV - Request data deletion

Last updated: November 10, 2026
App: CastTV for Android (package com.marouane.tvcast), by Marouane Studio, Morocco

1. CastTV has no accounts

CastTV has no sign-in, no profile and no account to delete. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and your videos, links and TV names are never uploaded to us. Settings and recent devices stay on your phone.

2. Who may hold data

Some services used by the app may hold data linked to random identifiers, not to your name. The Privacy Policy explains each one in detail.

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): usage statistics, only if "Share usage statistics" is on. Linked to an app instance identifier. Deleted automatically after 2 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports, only if "Share crash reports" is on. Linked to a Crashlytics installation identifier. Deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Google AdMob: in the free version, ad data linked to your device's advertising ID, handled by Google under its own policies.
  • RevenueCat: if you bought CastTV Premium, your subscription status (plan, dates, price and the Google Play purchase token), linked to a random identifier. The purchase itself is in your Google Play account.
  • Us: emails you sent to support, if any.

3. Delete what is on your phone

To delete everything CastTV keeps on your phone (settings, recent devices, privacy choices, the stored Premium state), open your phone's Settings > Apps > CastTV > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall CastTV. Clearing storage also gives the app new random identifiers, so new data is no longer linked to the old ones.

Uninstalling does not cancel a Premium subscription: cancel it in the Google Play Store app, in Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

4. Turn off usage statistics and crash reports

In CastTV, open Settings > Privacy and turn off Share usage statistics and Share crash reports. Collection stops at once, and crash reports still waiting on your phone are deleted. Where the law requires it, the same section has Ad privacy options to change your choice about personalized ads.

5. Reset or delete your advertising ID

Your advertising ID belongs to your phone, not to CastTV. To reset or delete it, open your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones Settings > Privacy > Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. After that, ads can no longer be linked to the old ID.

6. Ask us to delete your data

Write to tvcastsupport@gmail.com with the subject "CastTV data deletion request".

Email a deletion request

Because CastTV has no accounts, please tell us what to look for: if you had Premium, the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA." and is in your Google Play receipt email). We delete everything we can link to you: support emails, your RevenueCat subscription record, and the Firebase data we can find. We may ask a question to confirm the request is yours.

Response time: we answer and complete the deletion within 30 days. Records that the law requires us to keep (for example for accounting) are kept only as long as required.
